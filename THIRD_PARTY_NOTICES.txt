Resolved dependencies and remaining review
-----------------------------------------
Cargo.lock, studio/package-lock.json, and docker/requirements-whisper.txt record
Rust, Node, and Python inputs. Scan evidence is described in docs/security-baseline.md.
Rust static linking and Studio frontend bundling require resolved-dependency
copyright/license/NOTICE collection before redistributing binaries or web bundles.
The package lists and Trivy SBOMs do not replace those required texts.

MiniMax H3 weights are operator-provisioned, not shipped. Hosted OpenRouter/model
services and generated outputs are subject to provider-specific terms, not
Reel Maestro's license. Other repository media/design asset provenance remains
an explicit review item. See docs/security-baseline.md for release gates.
